# hr_analytics_dashboard
HR Analytics Dashboard
An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard designed to explore employee attrition and workforce trends across departments. The dashboard presents key HR metrics and visualizations to make employee data easier to understand and support data-informed HR discussions.
Project Overview
Employee attrition can affect workforce planning, recruitment, and employee retention. This project brings key workforce metrics into one interactive dashboard so users can explore attrition patterns by age, education, salary slab, job role, and years at the company.
The dashboard includes department navigation for:
- Human Resources
- Research & Development
- Sales
Objectives
- Present important workforce KPIs in one place.
- Explore employee attrition across demographic and job-related categories.
- Compare attrition patterns by salary slab, job role, age group, education, and tenure.
- Provide an interactive way to navigate department-level HR views.
- Make HR data easier to interpret through charts and summary metrics.
Dashboard Highlights
Key Performance Indicators
- Overall Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Salary
- Average Years at Company
Visualizations
- Attrition by Education: Shows how attrition is distributed across education categories.
- Attrition by Age: Compares attrition across age groups.
- Attrition by Salary Slab: Shows attrition across salary ranges.
- Attrition by Years at Company: Explores how attrition varies with employee tenure.
- Attrition by Job Role: Compares attrition counts across job roles.
- Job Role Summary: Provides a role-based breakdown across the displayed categories.
